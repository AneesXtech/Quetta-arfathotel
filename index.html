Our Story

A Karachi Legacy
Brewed With Love

Founded in 1998, Quetta Arfat Hotel has become a beloved institution in Karachi. Our Branch 3 on Shaheed-e-Millat Road welcomes thousands of guests daily, continuing a legacy of authentic Pakistani chai and warm hospitality.

Every cup is brewed with time-tested family recipes passed down through generations — premium tea leaves, authentic spices and pure ingredients. More than a chai hotel, we're a cultural hub where friendships are forged and traditions honoured.
